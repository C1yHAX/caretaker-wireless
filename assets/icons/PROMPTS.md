# Funkstrom-Icons – Erstrelease 1.0.0

Erstellt mit dem eingebauten Bildbearbeitungswerkzeug (image_gen), transparenter Hintergrund. Die gerenderten Spielmodelle dienen als Referenz. Das Senderbild erhält ausgehende orange Funkwellen, das Empfängerbild eingehende blaue Funkwellen. Ein IconBuilder formatiert die PNG-Dateien als native UI-Texturen mit 256×256 Pixeln und Alpha.

## transmitter.png

Use case: precise-object-edit. Create a polished square transparent inventory icon of the wireless POWER TRANSMITTER from the game The Last Caretaker, based accurately on the depicted slender antenna device. Image 1 is the engine-rendered whole transmitter model (use its silhouette and physical components); image 2 is a closeup gameplay view of that same slender device at the CENTER (use the front-panel detail, ignore all the large cylindrical machinery behind it). Isolate only this device and recenter to fill the square comfortably. Three-quarter front view, tall slim dark industrial metal antenna tower, narrow vertical illuminated panel, long antenna rods, compact power socket and metal mounting foot. Neutral studio light, realistic worn metal and crisp readable silhouette consistent with 3D rendered game inventory icons. Add a bold amber/orange outgoing-radio-waves glyph beside the upper body, clearly expanding AWAY from the antenna, with one outward arrow pointing right. The glyph must be large and readable at 64px. No labels, no lettering, no background rectangle, no surrounding machines or cables, no invented large satellite dish, no extra devices. Real transparent alpha background. Device and amber transmission glyph together centered and entirely in frame.

## receiver.png

Use case: precise-object-edit. Create a polished square transparent inventory icon of the wireless POWER RECEIVER from the game The Last Caretaker, accurately based on the depicted engine-rendered receiver model. Preserve the actual short squat form: wide square beveled grey metal mounting plate and a large dark blue/black circular electric socket on top. Recenter the device and fill the square comfortably, three-quarter elevated view, realistic worn metal, neutral studio light, crisp 3D game inventory render. Add a bold cyan/blue receiving-radio-waves glyph above the socket, with a prominent downward arrow pointing INTO the socket to indicate incoming power. The glyph must be large and clearly legible at 64px. The receiver must look compact and squat, NOT like a tall antenna/transmitter. No labels or letters, no backdrop rectangle, no surrounding machines or cables, no extra devices. Real transparent alpha background. Device and cyan receiving glyph together centered and entirely in frame.
